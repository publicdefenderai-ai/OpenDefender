## ca-conspiracy-182-a1

An agreement by two or more people to commit a crime under §182(a)(1), with the required criminal intent and an overt act under §184. The charging document must identify the target crime; agreement alone does not supply all statutory requirements.

For a target felony, §182 generally uses that felony’s punishment, with degree and multiple-target rules; conspiracy to murder uses first-degree murder punishment. Other covered conspiracies can carry up to 364 days (§18.5), §1170(h) imprisonment ordinarily for 16 months, 2 years, or 3 years, a base fine up to $10,000, or both. A felony identity-theft conspiracy permits a fine up to $25,000. There is no single accurate maximum without the target offense and statutory branch; do not treat every conspiracy as a felony with one generic term.
