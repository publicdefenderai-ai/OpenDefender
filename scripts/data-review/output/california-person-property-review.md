# California person/property expansion

Proposed additions: 26. Configured selectable after merge: 125 (previously 99). Source acquisition remains September 24, 2026. No production seed or deployment is certified.

The four-group batch accounts for 142 sections. Reuses 39 retained sections and acquires 118 additional sections for the batch and dependencies. Accounting is not full substantive verification of all 142 sections.

## Proposed exact identities

English text is used as an intentional fallback in all locales until translations are reviewed. Penalties are adult base terms, not total sentence predictions. Attorney review remains pending. Use the primary source and dependency list together.

### Assault with corrosive chemicals or flammable substances: §244

Willfully and maliciously placing or throwing a listed corrosive acid, caustic chemical or flammable substance on another person, with intent to injure the flesh or disfigure the body. The statute defines flammable substances; this is not every chemical exposure.

Mental state: Willful and malicious conduct with intent to injure or disfigure.

Felony: 2, 3, or 4 years in state prison. These are adult base penalties, not a prediction of the total sentence. Additional fines, restitution, assessments and applicable enhancements require separate review.

Dependencies: PEN:17, PEN:18.5, PEN:672.

[Official primary section](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=244.)

### Assault with a stun gun or less lethal weapon: §244.5(b)

An assault on another person using a stun gun or a less lethal weapon as defined by Penal Code section 16780. Assault requires an unlawful attempt and present ability to apply violent force; completed injury is not required.

Mental state: Willful act with awareness of facts that would lead a reasonable person to realize it would directly and probably result in force; present ability is required. Intent to injure is not required.

Felony: 16 months, 2 years, or 3 years under section 1170(h); misdemeanor alternative: up to 364 days in county jail. Section 17 governs misdemeanor treatment. These are adult base penalties, not a prediction of the total sentence. Additional fines, restitution, assessments and applicable enhancements require separate review.

Dependencies: PEN:17, PEN:18.5, PEN:672, PEN:240, PEN:16780, PEN:1170.

[Official primary section](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=244.5.)

Mental state: Willful act with awareness of facts that would lead a reasonable person to realize it would directly and probably result in force; present ability is required. Intent to injure is not required. The specified knowledge or reason to know victim status and duty is also required.

Felony: 2, 3, or 4 years under section 1170(h); misdemeanor alternative: up to 364 days in county jail. Section 17 governs misdemeanor treatment. These are adult base penalties, not a prediction of the total sentence. Additional fines, restitution, assessments and applicable enhancements require separate review.

Dependencies: PEN:17, PEN:18.5, PEN:672, PEN:240, PEN:16780, PEN:1170, PEN:830, PEN:245.1.

### Shooting at an inhabited dwelling or occupied vehicle: §246

Maliciously and willfully firing at an inhabited dwelling, occupied building, occupied motor vehicle or aircraft, or an inhabited housecar or camper. Inhabited means currently used as a dwelling, even if nobody is inside at that moment.

Mental state: Malicious and willful discharge at a qualifying target.

The statute calls the offense a felony and provides 3, 5, or 7 years in state prison, with a 6-month to 364-day county-jail alternative. Section 17 governs when misdemeanor treatment is available. These are adult base penalties, not a prediction of the total sentence. Additional fines, restitution, assessments and applicable enhancements require separate review.

Dependencies: PEN:17, PEN:18.5, PEN:672, VEH:362, VEH:243.

[Official primary section](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=246.)
