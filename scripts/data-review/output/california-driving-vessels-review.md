# California driving and vessels: combined review packet

215 candidate sections and dependencies: 40 reused, 174 newly retained, 1 absent probe. 30 independent instructions: 4 bounded catalog matches, 7 context/allegation entries, 19 unpublished branch entries. These are research counts, not a coverage percentage or count of new offenses.

- Research acquisition does not add catalog choices, approve penalties or extend any freshness receipt.
- Six discovery candidate groups and explicit probes are not complete Vehicle Code or vessel coverage.
- Same-section catalog associations are navigation aids only. Subdivision, actor and sentencing review remain required.
- VEH 23564 is an absent research probe, not a confirmed statutory error or repeal finding.
- Unknown or serious severity is never deferred as a minor misdemeanor.

## Substantive review order

### Injury DUI and omitted DUI branches

Separate injury causation, alcohol/drug/commercial/passenger branches and prior-dependent punishment. Existing 23152(a)/(b)/(f)/(g) choices do not cover every branch.

Read together: VEH:23153, VEH:23152, VEH:23140.

### Hit-and-run and post-collision duties

Distinguish injury/death from property-only accidents, driver from controlling-owner duties, knowledge, and the separate manslaughter flight enhancement.

Read together: VEH:20001, VEH:20002, VEH:20003, VEH:20004.

### Evasion

Separate ordinary flight, reckless flight, serious injury/death, and opposite-direction flight. Retain pursuit-identification predicates and grade-specific terms.

Read together: VEH:2800.1, VEH:2800.2, VEH:2800.3, VEH:2800.4.

### Suspended licenses and court duties

Identify the suspension basis, knowledge, repeat branches, injury and court appearance duties. Do not copy one suspension penalty across the family.

Read together: VEH:14601, VEH:14601.1, VEH:14601.2, VEH:14601.3, VEH:14601.4, VEH:14601.5, VEH:12951, VEH:40508.

### Reckless driving and street racing

Separate ordinary reckless driving, injury branches, speed contests, exhibitions and assistance. Verify current subdivisions against the literal instruction headings.

Read together: VEH:23103, VEH:23104, VEH:23105, VEH:23109, VEH:23109.1.

### Vessels and maritime offenses

Review vessel DUI, injury, repeat consequences, refusal and procedural context, vessel manslaughter and taking vessels. Do not import Vehicle Code punishment by analogy.

Read together: HNC:655, HNC:655.1, HNC:655.2, HNC:655.3, HNC:655.4, HNC:655.5, HNC:655.6, HNC:655.7, PEN:192.5, PEN:193.8, PEN:499.

### Vehicle identification and chop shops

Read together: VEH:10801, VEH:10802, VEH:10803.
