### §3743.65: Illegal discharge: separate the alleged subsection

IDs: `oh-illegal-fireworks`.

**Proposed change:** The older entry mixes possession, sale and discharge, says 90 days with a $1,000 ceiling, and asserts an injury/property-damage escalation not established by these mappings. Scope the discharge label to B only after document matching, with separate G/H candidates; F is a different F5 offense. Never silently migrate the saved ID.

**Scope candidates:** B, G, H. **Grade:** B/G: M1; H: MM.

**Exceptions and dependencies:** 3743.45 permits qualified consumer use on specified dates with property permission and allows local restrictions; 3743.80 has chapter-wide exclusions. Licensed exhibitions also need their authorities checked.

**Before implementation:** Obtain licensing/exhibition and safety-rule dependencies, build location/date questions, and independently check the candidate. Local ordinance charges require their own identity and source.

### Shared sentencing defaults and overrides

Applies as a research topic to 51 assigned sections. This is not complete dependency closure.

### Covered lender roles and special fines

Applies as a research topic to 3 assigned sections. This is not complete dependency closure.

1321.01 defines the licensee and loan concepts for 1321.141; 1321.51 defines the registrant for 1321.592. Each prohibits a loan meeting either the amount threshold ($1,000 or less) or duration threshold (one year or less), plus evasion. 4712.01 defines the covered services, credit and exclusions for 4712.071; that offense uses any of three triggers, including credit strictly below $5,000. Each retains its special $100–$500 fine and express strict-liability provision.

**Remaining:** 4712.071 APR incorporates federal Truth in Lending law and implementing regulations, which are not certified here. Covered/excluded status needs the full statutory definition; lending chapters and federal exemptions are not exhaustively closed by this module.

Sources: [§1321.01](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-1321.01), [§1321.51](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-1321.51), [§4712.01](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-4712.01), [§1321.141](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-1321.141), [§1321.592](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-1321.592), [§4712.071](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-4712.071).
