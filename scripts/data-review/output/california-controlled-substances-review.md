# California controlled-substances expansion and independent benchmark

27 additional adult choices from 20 primary sections; 218 configured California choices in total. Source expiry and changed-source holds still apply. This report does not verify deployment.

104 candidate sections are accounted for: 20 with new bounded branches, 6 with existing entries, 77 still open, and one absent benchmark source. Open includes context and procedural provisions not yet fully dispositioned; it is not a count of missing crimes.

Reuses 23 sections and adds 98 sections/99 versions. The original acquisition clock is preserved. Both versions of HSC 11375 are retained; only their identical exclusion/list is used, with no asserted effective date or standalone publication.

## Proposed charge choices

### Possession or purchase of cocaine base for sale: HSC §11351.5

Unlawfully possessing cocaine base for sale, or purchasing it for that purpose. Possession for sale requires a usable amount, knowledge of its presence and controlled character, and intent that it be sold. An agreement to buy by itself does not establish possession.

Mental state: Knowing possession, awareness of the substance’s controlled character, and the specified unlawful purpose are required; merely being nearby is insufficient.

Felony: 2, 3, or 4 years under Penal Code section 1170(h). Terms under Penal Code section 1170(h) are ordinarily served in county jail, subject to its state-prison exclusions. Section 11372(a) separately permits an additional fine up to $20,000, not a substitute for the stated imprisonment. A drug-program fee up to $150 under section 11372.7 depends on ability to pay. Adult base penalties are shown; enhancements, restitution, assessments, and case-specific sentencing rules may change the result.

### Adult possession of cannabis above the statutory quantity limits: HSC §11357(b)(2)
