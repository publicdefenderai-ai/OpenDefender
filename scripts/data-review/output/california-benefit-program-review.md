## WIC 10980(d): Unauthorized blank SNAP authorizations with intent to defraud

Knowingly using, transferring, acquiring or possessing blank authorizations to participate in SNAP in a manner not authorized by the state program chapter, with intent to defraud. The prosecution must establish the applicable unauthorized conduct, not merely possession or an ordinary purchase using benefits. State and incorporated federal program rules govern authorization; this summary does not decide whether a particular transaction was authorized. Section 10980(j) can bar prosecution for specified CalWORKs or CalFresh overpayments when the county had qualifying eligibility-match information and failed to provide timely, adequate notice; the 45-day rule includes authorized-delay exceptions. It is not blanket immunity for every benefits transaction.

The stated felony custody term is 16 months, 2 years or 3 years under Penal Code section 1170(h), subject to its state-prison exclusions. Section 10980(d) permits a base fine up to $5,000 alone or with that imprisonment. The fine-only alternative also permits misdemeanor treatment under Penal Code section 17(b); no separate misdemeanor jail term is stated in this subdivision. Do not substitute the six-month penalty from subdivision (a). Restitution, assessments, benefit consequences and separate charges require individual assessment.

## WIC 10980(e): Forgery involving SNAP authorizations or CalFresh benefits

Counterfeiting or altering, or knowingly using, transferring, acquiring or possessing counterfeit or altered SNAP authorizations, CalFresh benefits or electronically transferred benefits in an unauthorized manner under the incorporated federal program laws or regulations. Section 10980(e) identifies this offense as forgery. The prosecution must establish the applicable unauthorized conduct, not merely possession or an ordinary purchase using benefits. State and incorporated federal program rules govern authorization; this summary does not decide whether a particular transaction was authorized. Section 10980(j) can bar prosecution for specified CalWORKs or CalFresh overpayments when the county had qualifying eligibility-match information and failed to provide timely, adequate notice; the 45-day rule includes authorized-delay exceptions. It is not blanket immunity for every benefits transaction.

Penal Code section 473(a) supplies the ordinary forgery alternatives: up to 364 days in county jail, or ordinarily 16 months, 2 years or 3 years under section 1170(h), subject to its state-prison exclusions. Section 473(b) has a narrower misdemeanor rule for its listed instruments worth $950 or less, with specified prior-conviction and joint identity-theft exceptions. Do not assume every benefit authorization qualifies as a listed instrument or apply that reduction from value alone. If no other offense-specific fine is prescribed, section 672 permits an additional discretionary base fine up to $1,000 for a misdemeanor or $10,000 for a felony. Restitution, assessments, benefit consequences and separate charges require individual assessment.

## WIC 10980(f): Public employee embezzlement of entrusted CalFresh benefits

Fraudulently appropriating CalFresh benefits, electronically transferred benefits or SNAP authorizations entrusted to the person through duties as a public employee. Section 10980(f) identifies this as embezzlement of public funds; it is not an ordinary recipient-overpayment charge. Section 10980(j) can bar prosecution for specified CalWORKs or CalFresh overpayments when the county had qualifying eligibility-match information and failed to provide timely, adequate notice; the 45-day rule includes authorized-delay exceptions. It is not blanket immunity for every benefits transaction.

Felony public-funds embezzlement under Penal Code section 514 carries state-prison punishment, ordinarily 16 months, 2 years or 3 years under section 18. Do not substitute the value-based petty-theft misdemeanor ceiling or section 1170(h) county-jail default for this public-funds branch. Section 514 also provides ineligibility thereafter for an office of honor, trust or profit in this state. If no other offense-specific fine is prescribed, section 672 permits an additional discretionary base fine up to $10,000. For (f) or (g) violations or attempts committed by electronic transfer, section 10980(h) specifies additional consecutive terms of 1, 2, 3 or 4 years when the transfer exceeds $50,000, $150,000, $1 million or $2.5 million respectively. Exactly a threshold does not exceed it. Common-scheme aggregation can apply; (i) bars an additional (h) term when another law imposes an additional term for the (f) or (g) violation. Restitution, assessments, benefit consequences and separate charges require individual assessment.

## WIC 10980(g)(1): Unauthorized CalFresh benefits or authorizations: $950 or less

Knowingly using, transferring, selling, purchasing or possessing covered CalFresh benefits, electronically transferred benefits or SNAP authorizations in a manner not authorized by the specified state or federal program laws. This branch applies when the face value of the benefits or authorizations is $950 or less, including exactly $950. The prosecution must establish the applicable unauthorized conduct, not merely possession or an ordinary purchase using benefits. State and incorporated federal program rules govern authorization; this summary does not decide whether a particular transaction was authorized. Section 10980(j) can bar prosecution for specified CalWORKs or CalFresh overpayments when the county had qualifying eligibility-match information and failed to provide timely, adequate notice; the 45-day rule includes authorized-delay exceptions. It is not blanket immunity for every benefits transaction.

Misdemeanor: up to six months in county jail, a base fine up to $500, or both. For (f) or (g) violations or attempts committed by electronic transfer, section 10980(h) specifies additional consecutive terms of 1, 2, 3 or 4 years when the transfer exceeds $50,000, $150,000, $1 million or $2.5 million respectively. Exactly a threshold does not exceed it. Common-scheme aggregation can apply; (i) bars an additional (h) term when another law imposes an additional term for the (f) or (g) violation. Restitution, assessments, benefit consequences and separate charges require individual assessment.
