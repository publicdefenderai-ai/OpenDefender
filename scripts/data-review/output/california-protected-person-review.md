### Assault on a custodial officer: §241.1

An unlawful attempt with present ability to apply violent force is required; actual contact or injury is not required. Assault on a custodial officer defined in section 831 or 831.5 while performing duties. The accused must know or reasonably be expected to know the specified status and performance of duties.

Mental state: Willful act with awareness of facts that would lead a reasonable person to realize it would directly and probably result in force, with present ability. Intent to injure is not required. The accused must know or reasonably be expected to know the specified status and performance of duties.

Felony alternative: 16 months, 2 years, or 3 years under section 1170(h); misdemeanor alternative: up to 364 days in county jail. Section 17 governs misdemeanor treatment. These are adult base penalties, not total sentence predictions. Restitution, assessments, enhancements and case-specific sentencing rules require separate review.

Dependencies: PEN:1170, PEN:17, PEN:18.5, PEN:240, PEN:672, PEN:831, PEN:831.5.
