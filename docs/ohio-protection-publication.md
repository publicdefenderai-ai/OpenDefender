## Incident dates and limits

The March 20, 2025 version of 2919.25 adds the presumption of prison in (D)(4). Section 2929.14(A)(3)(a) includes domestic violence on its special F3 list, with terms from 12 to 60 months. The choice describes the separate mandatory terms in pregnancy cases. It does not treat every domestic violence conviction as a felony.

The September 30, 2025 version of 2917.211 establishes separate fabricated-image branches and felony grades. The preceding version graded private-image dissemination as M3, M2 or M1 depending on qualifying priors, and included an age-specific prosecution bar. Both prior statutes are retained separately from operative evidence.

The reporting choices do not classify voluntary reports, authorized disclosures or agency procedures as offenses. Other juvenile-record provisions referenced by 2151.99 concern a separate subject. Existing child-endangerment, juvenile-registration and criminal-trespass holds remain intact.
