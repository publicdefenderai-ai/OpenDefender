# New York assault and injury research

Research-only mapping of **18 conduct alternatives** across Penal Law 120.05, 120.10 and 121.12. Another **ten specialized alternatives in 120.05 are explicitly held** for membership, duty, location and incorporated-dependency review. This is partial substantive coverage of 120.05, not a claim that its entire section is publication-ready.

The packet retains 24 official Senate statutory documents: 18 reused with byte-identical original acquisition metadata and six newly acquired on November 22, 2026. Four mappings are Class B violent felonies and fourteen are Class D violent felonies. No catalog, explanation, citation overlay, eligibility, receipt, database or live selection is changed.

## Meaning and sentencing boundaries

The rows preserve physical versus serious physical injury, intentional versus reckless injury, intentional firearm discharge with reckless injury, the medical-treatment/nonconsent conditions in 120.05(5), and the age predicates in 120.05(8), (9) and (12). Exactly ten years of age difference does not satisfy (12). The school-grounds definition in 220.00(14) is broader than campus property and includes specified areas within 1,000 feet; its employee and student branches remain distinct.

Felony assault requires injury during and in furtherance of the felony/attempt or immediate flight and excludes an injured participant. The Article 130 corroboration carveout is present in 120.05(6), supported by retained 130.16; it is not silently copied into 120.10(4), whose result is serious physical injury. The exact predicate and applicable judicial limitations remain case-specific prerequisites.

The correctional-facility reference in 120.05(7) is retained with COR 40(3), including its expressly article-limited secure-facility language. This packet does not expand that phrase into a general rule covering every youth or treatment facility.

Ordinary B violent sentencing under 70.02(3)(a) is **5–25 years plus 2.5–5 years of post-release supervision** under 70.45(2)(f). The publisher-labelled authorization effective until September 1, 2027 is bound separately from scheduled text.

The mapped D offenses are subject to 60.05(5) and 70.02(2)(b). **If state-prison commitment is imposed**, the ordinary determinate term is **2–7 years plus 1.5–3 years of post-release supervision** under 70.45(2)(e). This is not an unconditional mandatory two-year state-prison minimum. Sections 70.00 and 85.00, armed-felony plea conditions in 70.02(4) with CPL 1.20/220.10/220.30, and multiple-offender exceptions remain separate. No probation or alternative disposition is guaranteed. Individual fines under 80.00 retain the greater-of-$5,000-or-twice-gain rule; corporate penalties, restitution and fees are outside the summary.

## Judicial and temporal prerequisites

The official [120.10(3) CJI](https://www.nycourts.gov/judges/cji/2-PenalLaw/120/120-10%283%29.pdf), inspected through the browser, identifies *Feingold* and later explanatory revisions: ordinary reckless risk does not by itself establish depraved indifference. The mapped summary expressly preserves this separate mental-state issue. The local raw-PDF acquisition attempt returned HTTP 403, so the CJI is not counted among the 24 hash-bound statutory documents or represented as completed judicial review.

The official [121.12 CJI](https://www.nycourts.gov/judges/cji/2-PenalLaw/121/121.12.pdf), also inspected through the browser, separates the two obstruction acts, causation and intent and addresses the medical/dental affirmative defense. Its November 11, 2010 conduct-date label is a follow-up instruction reference, not a silently adopted independent enactment chronology. Instruction/source acquisition and applicable case-law limits still require completion before publication.

Original acquisition timestamps and publisher-page hashes are preserved; no legal commencement date is inferred from a page revision date.
