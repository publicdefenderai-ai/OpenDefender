# Florida child kidnapping and false imprisonment

The existing kidnapping choice covers the first three predicates in §787.01(3)(a), the conduct actually sampled in Florida’s sentencing chart. This packet adds its three remaining current predicate groups and the six aggravated false-imprisonment groups. The existing ordinary kidnapping and false-imprisonment records have separate IDs. The sentencing-chart sample still contains one kidnapping row. [Scope crosswalk](../scripts/data-review/florida-launch/scope-crosswalk.json)

| Predicate during restraint of a child under 13 | Kidnapping | False imprisonment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aggravated child abuse under §827.03(1)(a) | Existing grouped choice | §787.02(3)(a)1. |
| Sexual battery against the child under chapter 794 | Existing grouped choice | §787.02(3)(a)2. |
| Listed lewd or lascivious offense under §800.04 or §847.0135(5) | Existing grouped choice | §787.02(3)(a)3. |
| Forcing, compelling or coercing the child to become a prostitute under §796.04 | §787.01(3)(a)4. | §787.02(3)(a)4. |
| Exploitation or allowing exploitation in violation of §450.151 | §787.01(3)(a)5. | §787.02(3)(a)5. |
| Covered trafficking under §787.06(3)(g) or (5) | §787.01(3)(a)6. | §787.02(3)(a)6. |

The custody offense has its own elements and grade. Kidnapping requires one of the purposes in §787.01(1) and the aggravated offense is a life felony. Aggravated false imprisonment is ordinarily a first-degree felony punishable up to life. Each subsection (3)(b) permits separate judgments and sentences for the accompanying crimes. The statute labels one trafficking predicate capital. This label does not attach to either restraint charge. [§787.01](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/787.01) · [§787.02](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/787.02)

For conduct beginning October 1, 2025 the Legislature added §787.06(5) to both trafficking lists. The adult venture-management and sexual-exploitation elements, including the under-12 or mental-impairment condition, remain distinct from §787.06(3)(g). No independent capital sentencing advice is published by this packet. [Chapter 2025-156, §§5–7, 97](https://laws.flrules.org/2025/156)

Both restraint statutes expressly reference former §796.03 and §796.04. The former provision was repealed October 1, 2014. Historical conduct requires the applicable offense-date law. The current entries use only the §796.04 alternative. Section 796.001 expresses legislative intent to prosecute adults involving minors under other laws. The new entries identify the incorporated conduct in the chapter 787 offenses. [Chapter 2014-160, §§9–10, 33](https://laws.flrules.org/2014/160) · [§796.001](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/796.001) · [§796.04](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/796.04)
