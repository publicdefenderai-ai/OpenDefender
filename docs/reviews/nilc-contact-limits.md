# NILC contact limits

Checked November 27, 2026 against NILC's official [legal-assistance page](https://www.nilc.org/request-for-legal-assistance/) and [contact page](https://www.nilc.org/about-us/contact-us/).

The contact page lists 213-639-3900 as an office contact. The assistance page states that NILC does not accept legal-assistance requests by phone or return those voicemails, and generally does not provide direct immigration representation. An office listing therefore does not support presenting that number as an emergency or legal-help hotline.

The raids toolkit, encounter scenarios, checklist, and printable emergency card now point to assistance information and referrals with the service limits visible. The printed card includes the full URL so it remains useful on paper. English and Spanish copy is provided; the toolkit's existing labeled English fallback for Chinese sessions remains intact. Personal contact fields and HTML escaping are preserved.

Only the intentionally changed NILC text, contact-list headings, checklist item, and link are updated in the published-content fixture. Independent browser checks reject the NILC office number and assert the assistance URL and telephone limitation on screen and in print. This change does not certify other organizations' numbers or availability, promise a referral response, alter immigration eligibility, or modify production data.
