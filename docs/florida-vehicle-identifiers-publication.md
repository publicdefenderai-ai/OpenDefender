# Florida vehicle and vessel identification

The sentencing-chart sample uses §319.30(5) and §328.07(4). Each parent citation now finds two separately sourced choices. The former distinguishes a title or plate connected to an unlawful salvage sale from a plate removed from its original vehicle. The latter distinguishes counterfeit or reassigned plates or decals from knowingly dealing in a vessel or part with a compromised number. Entering a parent citation still requires choosing the alleged conduct. The frozen citation benchmark compares primary codes literally and therefore records these as same-section candidates.

The eight records also cover possession of a vehicle with its plate removed, counterfeit vehicle plates or decals, and obtaining or passing the title to a stolen vehicle or vessel. Vehicle-repair and approved-replacement exceptions remain attached to their respective branches. Where forfeiture applies, seizure and forfeiture proceedings are separate from the criminal grade. The source for the §319.30(5) penalty is the general provision in (10). Chapter 2026-56 amended insurer signature controls in §319.30(3)(d) effective July 1, 2026; the amendment did not change the elements of the sampled offense. [Chapter 2026-56, §§1–2](https://laws.flrules.org/2026/56)

Each choice uses the 2026 official edition and has its own grade, statute bindings and three-language guidance. Unrelated salvage-dealer duties, display infractions and inspection authority are outside this publication.
