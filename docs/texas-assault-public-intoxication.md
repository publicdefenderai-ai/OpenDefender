# Texas bodily-injury assault and public intoxication

Three DPS codes in the 2025 adult-arrest benchmark represent 69,464 reported arrests: 44,649 family bodily-injury assaults, 14,591 bodily-injury assaults and 10,224 family or household assaults involving impaired breathing or circulation. The five assault choices separately identify ordinary bodily injury, ordinary family bodily injury, a qualifying prior conviction, the specified breathing or circulation conduct, and the combination of that conduct with a qualifying prior. The last branch requires all three predicates in §22.01(b-3), including the qualifying relationship. Threat and offensive-contact offenses and §22.02 aggravated assault require their own review. The generic domestic-violence ID remains held. [Texas Penal Code §22.01](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/PE/htm/PE.22.htm#22.01)

The corrected public-intoxication ID requires intoxication to a degree that may endanger the person or another, preserves the statutory medical-treatment defense, and distinguishes ordinary adult punishment, the repeat-offense provision and the separate under-21 rules. [Texas Penal Code §49.02](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/PE/htm/PE.49.htm#49.02)

Thirteen official sections from six TCSS chapters were retrieved November 13, 2026 Pacific. Their exact section text, official heading, section effective-date metadata, retrieval instant, chapter hash and both normalized-text hash conventions are retained in the evidence file. Each catalog row specifies the conduct and grading subdivisions and applicable sentencing or definition sources. The current text of §22.01 reflects amendments effective September 1, 2025; this review does not reconstruct the law for earlier offense dates. Individual qualification of a particular prior conviction, collateral consequences and case-specific sentencing enhancements are outside these summaries.

The prepared manifest has 117 Texas catalog rows, 40 eligible choices, 77 held rows and 47 distinct sources. Four IDs are new; the exact family bodily-injury ID becomes eligible and the existing public-intoxication ID receives corrected guidance. The other 111 records are unchanged. Deployment before an approved import would withhold the previously available public-intoxication ID where the old ingestion lacks its dependencies.
