## Versions and limits

- The Ohio Legislative Service Commission presents 2907.06 effective March 21, 2025 as a composite with one conduct paragraph. The immediately preceding version included five numbered circumstances. Earlier alleged conduct must use its applicable version. Former 2907.12 remains named as a prior-offense predicate in (C); the current publisher has no section at that number. No replacement is inferred.
- The August 9, 2024 version of 2907.07 retains the nonspouse condition in (B)(1), but removed it from the trafficking-victim branch in (B)(2). The electronic branches retain the transmission's Ohio origin or receipt requirement. The mandatory F2 sentencing text explicitly distinguishes conduct before March 22, 2019 from later conduct.
- The April 4, 2023 version of 2907.08 changes the elements of (B)/(C) and adds recording above clothing in (D). The felony branch is (C), despite its penalty appearing after the penalty for (D).
- Each choice cites Chapter 2950 definitions and registration provisions for a separate consequence assessment. The entries do not assign registration tiers or durations to an individual. The custody and fine schedules are adult schedules.
- Three dated historical sources are retained outside the current publication packet. Existing legal decisions and holds remain intact.
