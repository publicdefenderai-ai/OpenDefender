# Texas handgun carrying branches

Ten choices extend the earlier review of Penal Code §46.02. Together with intentional public display and intoxicated carry from PR 232, twelve choices now describe specific conduct with the ordinary Class A grade. The five-year clock for a prior offense runs from the date the person committed it. A conviction must already exist when the later carrying occurs. Vehicle and watercraft choices require that the person own or control the vehicle or watercraft. All ten choices retain §§46.01 and 46.15 and the misdemeanor punishment provisions. | §46.02(a-1)(1) | Unholstered handgun in plain view, age 21 or older | Age 21 or older is the scope of this choice. It supplies no finding about younger people. |
| §46.02(a-1)(2)(A) | Carrying during criminal activity in the vehicle or watercraft | Class C violations of traffic or boating laws or ordinances are excluded. |
| §46.02(a-1)(2)(B) | Firearm possession prohibited under §46.04(a), (b) or (c) | Separate choices require the actual felony prohibition, qualifying family/household assault prohibition or qualifying unexpired order after notice. The record alone cannot establish a person's legal status, any relief from a disability or the effect of an individual order. Other claimed prohibitions require independent support. |
