# Ohio physical control, dangerous driving and wrongful entrustment

Thirteen selections across six primary sections bind the conduct, grade, exceptions and sanctions separately.

| Section | Selections | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 4511.194 | 3 | Physical control while impaired, at a prescribed alcohol concentration, or at a listed controlled-substance or metabolite concentration. Each requires the driver's position and possession of the ignition key or device. A properly obtained and used prescription is relevant to the third branch only. |
| 4511.20 and 4511.201 | 2 | Willful or wanton disregard, separated by street/highway versus other public or private property. The permitted-competition exception belongs to the latter. The statute’s one-year prior-offense rules and the separate discretionary reckless-operation suspension are distinguished. |
| 4511.202 | 1 | Failure to maintain reasonable control of a vehicle or agricultural tractor, including a specified towed unit, on the public-traffic locations specified. Minor misdemeanor. |
| 4511.203 | 5 | Entrusting a vehicle to a driver lacking licensing, under suspension, prohibited by financial responsibility law, driving under OVI law, or barred by an immobilization waiver order. Each requires knowledge or reasonable cause to believe. The first three ordinarily have no jail and a specified repeat increase; the other two are M1 without a prior. The discretionary vehicle sanctions have a separate prior-offense schedule. |
| 4511.251 | 2 | Knowing participation and rendering assistance to racing, stunt driving or street takeover. The October 24, 2024 text defines the covered conduct and location, a permitted-competition exception, and a mandatory 30-day to three-year license suspension. |

Section 4511.75 was also screened and retained. Division (F)(1) supplies a fine of up to $500 for violating (A) and requires a court appearance. The local penalty text does not assign an ordinary misdemeanor degree or settle the relationship to the separate bus-driver duties. No selection is proposed for that section.

The 13 new choices bring the reviewed Ohio catalog on this branch from 428 to 441 identities. The retained publication uses 23 official sections and 202 source spans. The existing section 4511.19 OVI choices and prior holds are preserved. Legacy identities are not redirected to a new subdivision. Spanish and Chinese requests carry the English fallback notice. Production availability depends on separately verified operational requirements.
