## Time-sensitive source context

The current 2919.24(B)(3) reaches conduct contributing to habitual truancy, and (C) says an unruliness or delinquency adjudication is unnecessary. From April 6, 2017 through October 8, 2026, (B)(3) instead required conduct contributing to a delinquency adjudication based on violation of a court order adjudicating the child unruly for habitual truancy. M1 and the separate-day rule continued through the October 9, 2026 amendment. The change appears in the affected choice, alongside the grade.

The referenced truancy-policy provision in 3313.663 also changed October 9, 2026. Its prior version required central posting in each school. The current version allows posting on the district website as an alternative. Both official historical versions are retained with dated URLs and hashes in the historical-context file. They are excluded from the current statutory document map.
