# Ohio liquor and identification charges

Eighteen individually cited choices cover eight sections. The definitions use the official heading with the conduct subdivision in the citation. The new grade evidence comes from § 4301.99.

| Primary statute | Choices | Grade and scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 4301.631(C) through (I) | 7 | M4. The definition of low-alcohol beverage and the under-18 threshold apply throughout. The three branches applicable only to underage actors display juvenile disposition guidance. The presence, accompaniment and exceptions for provision of a beverage differ by subdivision. |
| 4301.634 | 1 | Under-21 identifying information at a qualifying sales location. The specific fines and possible license consequences under § 4301.99(F) depend on whether qualifying identification was presented and the number of qualifying violations. |
| 4301.636(A)(1), (A)(2), (B)(1), (B)(2) | 4 | F5. Age-identification and driver-license branches have different conduct, markings, compensation conditions and exceptions. |
| 4301.64 | 1 | M4 for consumption in a motor vehicle. Section 4301.62(D) defines the particular limousine exception. Section 4301.99(B) separately addresses juvenile licensing and community service. |
| 4301.65(B), (C) | 2 | Sale or offer is ordinarily M3, increasing to M1 for the specified repeat within five years. Purchase or use is a minor misdemeanor. |
| 4301.66, 4301.67, 4301.68 | 3 | Obstructing inspection is M1 and excludes a bona fide private residence. Unlawful possession is M4 and has a one-liter threshold for spirituous liquor. The original-container offense is M1. |

The direct-purchase branches in §§ 4301.63 and 4301.631(B) have a separate fine under § 4301.99(E). They are excluded from this batch; the M4 classification for § 4301.631(C) through (I) does not extend to them. Existing choices and dispositions for §§ 4301.21, 4301.62, 4301.633, 4301.69 and 4301.74 retain their own treatment.
