**Spiny lobster.** Fewer than 100 can be charged separately per lobster under (5)(b)1.–2., subject to the combined four-year and $4,000 ceiling for a single scheme or course of conduct. At 100 the felony rule applies, with a mandatory civil fine of at least $500, a required administrative penalty up to $2,000 and possible license suspension up to 12 months. The different commercial-harvester provision in (2)(a) uses more than 100. Its $10 per illegal animal threshold excludes exactly 100. The Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission identifies a carapace length greater than three inches for ordinary harvest and a limited undersized-lobster allowance for qualifying commercial trap operations. Season closures, separated tails and detailed equipment or license eligibility require their own facts. A separately charged violation of (2)(d)1. carries a one-year custody minimum and $1,000 minimum fine. The records keep those minimums distinct from the specific §483.901 prosecution. [Florida Statutes §483.901](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/483.901) · [Florida Statutes §456.065](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/456.065)

The evidence retains the antique-possession defense, licensed gaming, qualifying shipments, amusement exceptions and exceptions specific to §849.231.
