# Workbook follow-up for L11

The November 17 export adds L11 and L12 decisions. L01 through L10 match the preceding export. Implementation does not establish attorney approval of individual state eligibility rules.

| Item | Change and authority |
| --- | --- |
| L11.1 | Ask about a waiting period only when the rule and its starting event are identified. The New York petition question uses the later of the last conviction or release from incarceration and accepts an unknown answer. [NY Courts, CPL 160.59](https://www.nycourts.gov/help/criminal/sealed-records-after-10-years-cpl-16059) |
| L11.2 | A published automatic program links to state guidance. Program existence alone does not produce an eligibility result. Missing periods remain unknown. The New York ten-year check concerns this particular petition pathway. Completing the wait alone does not establish eligibility. |
| L11.3 | Nonconviction cases ask how the case ended. Cannabis records ask about the offense and disposition. A complete New York acquittal points to possible sealing, with a court link for confirmation. Missing facts yield an undetermined result. [NY Courts, favorable dispositions](https://www.nycourts.gov/help/criminal/sealed-records-good-result-favorable-disposition-cpl-ss16050) |
| L11.4 | Revised reporting limits, notices and disputes, conditional disclosure, certificates and publisher-removal requests. The restored preparation page includes questions to take to legal aid. [Federal reporting limits](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/1681c), [dispute procedure](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/1681i), [FTC employer background checks](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/employer-background-checks-and-your-rights), [Ohio employment certificate](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2953.25), [California pardons](https://www.gov.ca.gov/clemency/pardons/), [California publication fees](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1798.91.1.&lawCode=CIV) |

Further state-by-state review is needed before enabling additional positive, timing or exclusion results. Legacy state reference material has not received a comprehensive state-by-state audit.
