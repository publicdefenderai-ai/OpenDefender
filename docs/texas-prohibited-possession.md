## Scope and chronology

The felony firearm window runs from conviction until the fifth anniversary of the later applicable confinement or supervision release. The post-window branch concerns possession away from the premises where the person lives. Neither branch says that reaching five years restores all firearm rights. The family-assault and gang-vehicle branches remain Class A misdemeanors in ordinary circumstances. Body armor has no imported five-year exception. The confined-weapon authorization defense remains attached to its own statute. Chapter 46 school-zone/function findings can increase the punishment category; the knowledge and beyond-reasonable-doubt requirements and relevant school definition are retained, without treating every case as enhanced.

Section 46.04(c)'s protective-order branches remain a separately scoped follow-up: this packet does not decide every order type, notice, expiry or exception. Separate federal firearm restrictions and individual constitutional challenges are not adjudicated by these Texas summaries.

Section 38.11's ordinary third-degree grade is separated from the correctional-employee second-degree branch and the new special first-degree employee/drug-death branch. The latter carries 15 to 99 years or life and a possible $250,000 fine, effective September 1, 2025, not an ordinary first-degree five-year/$10,000 profile. These employee enhancements apply only to (a)(1), (b) and (c), not every item or act. Civil-facility employment, a contractor label or alcohol alone does not silently satisfy the drug-death provision.

Prescription, delivery, clergy, authorized-weapon and civil-device exceptions are attached only where the statute supplies them. The clergy exception concerns no more than four ounces, the specified authorized ritual and personal consumption or removal. Tobacco provision to local-jail custody requires the actual prohibited-rule circumstance. The separate device-service acts require the intent to provide or make the device/component available. The statutory facility, drug, practitioner, tobacco and device definitions are retained individually.

The weapon-possession defense retains both official article 2A.001 versions; it does not exclude a legally qualifying officer merely because a prior selector elsewhere was limited to common roles. That defense does not publish a new set of officer offense identities. Explicit statutory intent and the general voluntary-conduct/culpability provisions are preserved. The packet does not represent a disputed blanket mental-state instruction as settled law.

The special attempt grade in §38.11(h) is disclosed and bound to §15.01. No separate attempt choices are published here. Existing holds and previously answered decisions are preserved.
