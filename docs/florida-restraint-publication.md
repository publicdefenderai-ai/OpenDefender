For kidnapping alleged to facilitate another felony, the Florida Supreme Court limits charges based on confinement incidental to the other offense. This limitation concerns the felony-facilitation theory in §787.01(1)(a)2. [Faison v. State](https://law.justia.com/cases/florida/supreme-court/1983/60792-0.html) and [Berry v. State](https://law.justia.com/cases/florida/supreme-court/1996/85540-0.html).
