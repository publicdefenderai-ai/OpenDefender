## Published court context and instruction limits

A separate court-context artifact retains the actual text of *Owens v. State* (Texas Court of Criminal Appeals, June 4, 2025) and *Counterman v. Colorado* (U.S. Supreme Court, June 27, 2023), with source URLs, acquisition times, PDF/text hashes and exact page-span bindings. Owens is the actual opinion hosted by Justia, identified as a mirror; Counterman is the official Supreme Court PDF. Tests recompute every retained text and span hash and verify each row's context keys.

Owens supplies an as-applied protected-expression limitation, not a categorical assertion that the electronic-harassment statute is invalid. True-threat guidance distinguishes subjective awareness of threatening character from intent merely to annoy or harass and from an objective listener standard. These limits accompany the relevant product descriptions rather than being hidden only in this document.

Publisher CPJC 42.9 (edition 055) was inspected but is not mapped to these new stalking scopes: its retained formulation does not establish an appropriately current instruction for the complete published scope and constitutional boundary. No new instruction reference is published for these 28 records. This is an unverified mapping, not a claim that no relevant instruction exists.
