## Repeat-offender sentencing

Section 2925.041(C)(1) prescribes a mandatory term of at least five years for a qualifying F3 methamphetamine offense with the specified combination of prior convictions or guilty pleas. The Supreme Court of Ohio reinstated the five-year term in *State v. Pribble*, 158 Ohio St.3d 490, 2019-Ohio-4808. The public citation record contains the publisher URL, decision date and PDF hash. The entry explicitly distinguishes this exception from the ordinary 9–36-month F3 terms. [Supreme Court opinion](https://www.supremecourt.ohio.gov/rod/docs/pdf/0/2019/2019-Ohio-4808.pdf)
