# Review confidentiality

Keep attorney questions, proposed legal advice, and deliberative attorney-review materials out of tracked files, commits, pull request descriptions, issues, and public reports. Share those materials directly with the owner or in local-only files outside the tracked repository. Do not assume that a label, local storage, or an AI conversation establishes legal privilege.

Public source texts, published product content, reproducible technical checks, and neutral publication/hold accounting may remain in the repository. Do not copy private deliberations into those artifacts. Do not rewrite published Git history without explicit owner authorization.
